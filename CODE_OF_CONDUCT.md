# Community conduct

Editors, translators, caption users and developers are welcome. Use the names people choose, explain technical terms and critique the work. Share examples only with permission. Harassment, threats, private information disclosure and discriminatory abuse are grounds for moderation.

This applies to repository issues, reviews, discussions and events organized for this project. A maintainer may edit or hide identifying information, remove abusive content, warn a participant, or restrict repeated or severe abuse. Briefly document the reason without exposing the reporter. Affected contributors may request a second maintainer review when one is available.

For an ordinary moderation request, mention the maintainer on the thread without repeating the harmful content. For an incident that needs privacy, use GitHub's private “Report abuse” flow on the account or content. Never post another person's private evidence in an issue.
